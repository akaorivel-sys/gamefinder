## Quality architecture
- 400 Enriched games keep the detailed filters and editorial metadata.
- 9,600 Enriched Core slots are populated from a real Steam game AppID/title source at runtime.
- Enriched Core does not invent genres, Japanese support, system requirements, or summaries.
- 30 existing expert articles remain the specialist layer.
- 170 new Data Guides compare the already-curated 400-game database rather than mass-writing fake per-game expertise.
- Data Guides are distinct filter/comparison pages and disclose their methodology.

## Why the 10,000 games are not 10,000 SEO pages
- Creating 9,600 thin individual pages would repeat the exact quality problem GameFinder was rebuilt to avoid.
- Enriched Core entries are searchable but link to Steam until GameFinder has enough verified information to promote them to Enriched.
